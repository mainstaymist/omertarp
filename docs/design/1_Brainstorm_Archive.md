# Mafia RP — Complete Brainstorm Archive
Version: 0.1.0
Last updated: 2026-07-26

## Purpose

This file preserves every important concept discussed for the Garry's Mod Mafia RP gamemode, including ideas that remain tentative. It is the broad creative record. Finalized decisions belong in the Game Design Document.

## 1. Core Vision

A month-long organized-crime simulation inspired by Italian-American Mafia fiction and history, including *The Godfather*, rather than a conventional DarkRP job server.

Players build one of two competing institutions:

- Crime families build wealth, influence, loyalty, businesses, intelligence networks, and political protection.
- Police build public trust, case files, informant networks, departmental capability, and major prosecutions.

The game emphasizes:

- Earned information
- Social trust
- Institutional progression
- Physical evidence
- Consequential violence
- Seasonal stories
- Minimal omniscient UI
- Low reliance on open-world scale
- Systems achievable within Garry's Mod

Guiding rule:

> A player should not know something their character could not reasonably know.

## 2. Seasonal Structure

- Each season lasts approximately four weeks.
- A player chooses one principal allegiance for the season:
  - Crime-family path
  - Police path
  - Independent/civilian path
- Players cannot freely switch factions through a menu.
- Resignation, expulsion, defection, corruption, becoming an informant, or witness protection are possible narrative transitions with consequences.
- At season end:
  - Territories reset.
  - Businesses return to neutral or baseline ownership.
  - Family treasuries reset.
  - Investigations close or enter the archive.
  - Seasonal ranks reset.
  - Informants may be revealed.
  - Newspapers, major cases, deaths, leaders, and awards are archived.
- Account-level persistence should remain modest:
  - Cosmetics
  - Titles
  - Career history
  - Seasonal statistics
  - Quality-of-life unlocks
- Persistent unlocks must not create major combat or economic advantages.

## 3. Crime Families

### Persistent institutions

The city should contain up to four predefined crime-family institutions every season.

Reasons:

- The server cannot launch with no families because nobody can afford one.
- A founder disconnecting cannot destroy the organization.
- Low population does not delete the faction structure.
- Stable family identities create long-term community rivalries and history.
- Joke and disposable family creation is avoided.

Players compete to control existing institutions rather than inventing arbitrary organizations.

### Suggested hierarchy

- Independent
- Prospect / connected outsider
- Associate
- Soldier
- Caporegime
- Underboss
- Don

### Promotion principles

Promotion is based on:

- Loyalty
- Reliability
- Money or assets produced for the family
- Operational success
- Protecting members and property
- Sponsorship
- Leadership approval
- Reputation inside the family

It is not based on generic XP or combat statistics.

### Recruitment

Recommended structure:

- Family members may identify and employ outsiders.
- Higher ranks may sponsor prospects and associates.
- Capos may recommend formal membership.
- The Don or delegated acting leadership approves sensitive promotions.
- The Don should not be required online for every routine recruit.
- An Underboss or designated acting leader receives temporary authority during absences.
- Important recruitment and promotion decisions are recorded in a family ledger.

A formal initiation ceremony may accompany becoming a soldier or “made” member.

### Character names and family names

- Characters choose a first and last name at season start.
- Full names must be unique within the active season.
- Names are locked for that character.
- Crime-family membership does not change a character's surname.
- A character named Anthony Russo may belong to the Marino Family.
- Family affiliation is an organization, not a shared literal surname.

## 4. Character Identity and Introductions

### Default identity state

Every unfamiliar player appears as:

> Unknown

No Steam name, character name, faction, occupation, rank, or server-list identity is exposed.

### Introduction system

- Players introduce themselves in person.
- Identity knowledge is stored per observing character.
- A person known to one player may remain Unknown to everyone else.
- An introduction reveals the name the character provides, not their family.
- Family affiliation must be inferred, discovered, confessed, or reported.
- Sharing one's identity with a rival family is a meaningful operational-security failure.
- Aliases and nicknames may exist, but should not erase legal identity without consequences.

### Hidden population

- No conventional scoreboard.
- No list of connected players.
- No faction counts.
- Ideally, no exact server population in ordinary gameplay.
- No kill feed.
- No global join/leave announcements.
- No UI indicator showing who is speaking globally.

This prevents players from correlating real-world login patterns with robberies, murders, family activity, or police presence.

## 5. Disguises and Witness Descriptions

Characters can conceal or alter recognizable traits through:

- Hats
- Scarves
- Bandanas
- Masks
- Raised collars
- Coats
- Gloves
- Clothing changes

Possible witness descriptors:

- Approximate height
- Build
- Clothing
- Hair
- Facial hair
- Visible scars
- Voice characteristics
- Mask type
- Vehicle
- Weapon type

Different concealment options have tradeoffs:

- Subtle disguise: less suspicious but incomplete concealment.
- Bandana: portable and moderately effective.
- Full mask or balaclava: strong concealment but immediately alarms civilians and may accelerate police response.
- Gloves reduce fingerprints.
- Clothing can later be changed or discarded.

NPCs report descriptions, not account or character names.

## 6. Information Economy

Information is a form of loot and organizational power.

Sources include:

- Newspapers
- Bartenders
- Taxi drivers
- Store owners
- Witnesses
- Funeral attendance
- Phone calls
- Police press releases
- Paid informants
- Corrupt officials
- Rival-family moles
- Business records
- Surveillance
- Direct observation
- Rumors
- Stolen ledgers
- Shipment manifests
- Recorded conversations

A wealthy or disciplined family knows more because it invested in intelligence, not because the UI reveals more.

Sensitive information should be compartmentalized. A soldier should not automatically know every safehouse, business, treasury record, or plan.

Leadership may give different crews different information to identify leaks.

## 7. Daily Newspaper

The city publishes a black-and-white daily newspaper at the start of each in-game day or at a fixed multi-hour interval.

Visual direction:

- Period black-and-white typography
- Grainy photographs
- Strong front-page headline
- A black-and-white portrait or incident image for major deaths
- Physical newspaper props and readable interfaces in bars, homes, stations, and libraries

Example:

> MARIO SANTINO FOUND DEAD IN SKIPPY'S DELI

The article may include:

- Victim name if publicly identified
- Location
- Approximate time
- Police statement
- Publicly known circumstances
- Grainy black-and-white facial preview
- “No suspects” or a broad suspect description

The newspaper must not reveal:

- Secret family affiliations
- Killer identity without established public evidence
- Informant identities
- Private meetings
- Omniscient faction knowledge

Reports may be incomplete or occasionally inaccurate because they rely on public records, witnesses, and official statements.

Possible parallel publications:

- Public newspaper
- Internal police bulletin
- Family-created intelligence digest

## 8. Library and City Archive

The public library acts as the city's memory.

It archives:

- Every newspaper edition
- Famous murders
- Major robberies
- Police raids
- Trials
- Leadership changes
- Elections
- Public funerals
- Historic photographs
- Closed investigations
- Cold cases
- Seasonal awards

Players can browse previous seasons and follow all articles or cases associated with a notable character.

Possible exhibits:

- Longest-serving Don
- Most successful detective
- Largest robbery
- Largest seizure
- Most notorious assassination
- Wealthiest family
- Most infamous criminal
- Major unresolved case

## 9. Family Wealth and Supplies

Family wealth should purchase organizational capability rather than direct stat advantages.

Possible expenditures:

- Cell phones
- Radios
- Weapons
- Ammunition
- Medical supplies
- Vehicles
- Safehouse upgrades
- Disguises
- Burglary equipment
- Storage
- Surveillance equipment
- Counter-surveillance equipment
- Food and drink inventory
- Bribes
- Legal defense
- Funeral expenses
- Replacement equipment

Family equipment can:

- Be bought in bulk
- Be assigned by crew or rank
- Be tracked in a ledger
- Be stolen, lost, seized, or recovered
- Remain family property rather than permanent personal ownership

A family may need to choose between equipping everyone with phones, preparing a robbery crew, replacing a vehicle, or preserving emergency funds.

Avoid purchasable:

- Extra health
- Increased damage
- Permanent armor
- Movement bonuses
- Equipment that removes all operational risk

## 10. Communication

### No omniscient channels

Normal gameplay should not include:

- Global chat
- OOC chat
- Global family chat
- Omniscient police chat
- Discord-dependent operational coordination

Communication occurs through:

- Face-to-face voice
- Local text
- Phone calls
- Phone messages
- Written notes
- Meetings
- Messengers
- Police radios
- Specialized family radios, if purchased

### Cell phones

- Phones are physical inventory items.
- Each phone has a number.
- Numbers must be exchanged or discovered.
- Contacts are stored per character/device.
- Unknown callers appear as numbers.
- Phones can ring and expose a hidden person's presence.
- Phones can be stolen, searched, confiscated, replaced, tapped, or traced.
- Messages remain on the physical phone unless deleted.
- Disposable phones may be a later, costly option.
- Family leadership may issue devices selectively.

### Voice calls

- The holder hears the remote caller clearly.
- Nearby players can hear the local player's side of the call.
- Speakerphone can make the remote side audible.
- Private conversations require private physical spaces.
- Careless calls in bars, restaurants, streets, or social clubs can leak intelligence.

### Text accessibility

Players without microphones must remain fully capable of:

- Joining and leading families
- Joining and leading police
- Negotiating
- Making phone calls through text
- Sending messages
- Giving orders
- Testifying
- Running businesses
- Participating in robberies
- Acting as informants

Local text counts as spoken dialogue and is range-limited. It displays a known character name only to observers who know that identity; otherwise it displays Unknown.

## 11. No-HUD Philosophy

The game should have almost no permanent HUD.

Do not permanently display:

- Health
- Armor
- Money
- Hunger
- Player names
- Jobs
- Family affiliations
- Police count
- Server population
- Wanted level
- Crosshair
- Kill feed
- Minimap
- Objective markers
- Voice speaker list
- Global notifications

Contextual UI only:

- Stamina appears when low or draining.
- Injury indicators appear when medically necessary.
- Interaction prompts appear while targeting usable objects.
- Inventory appears only when opened.
- Phone UI appears while using a phone.
- Weapon ammunition is checked through inspection rather than a permanent counter.
- Money is checked using a wallet, register, bank record, or ledger.

## 12. Inventory and Physicality

- A dedicated inventory key opens a compact interface.
- The world does not pause.
- Items have size, slot, or concealment cost.
- Large weapons cannot be hidden easily.
- Clothing affects concealment or carrying capacity.
- Bags, briefcases, vehicle trunks, and storage furniture expand capacity.
- Items can be dropped, stolen, planted, searched, seized, or marked as family property.
- A temporary radial or compact item selector can appear only during active selection.

## 13. Businesses and Social Spaces

Businesses are operational assets, not passive money printers.

Examples:

- Restaurant: meetings, laundering, food, respectable cover
- Speakeasy/bar: drinking, gambling, rumors, recruitment, relaxation
- Social club: headquarters, treasury, ceremonies, leadership meetings
- Auto shop: vehicle work, chop-shop activity, hidden storage
- Funeral home: funerals, discreet transport, body-related services
- Tailor: clothing, disguises, suit repair
- Jeweler: valuables, laundering, fencing
- Doctor/clinic: quiet wound treatment, potential reporting risk
- Construction company: equipment, contracts, cover operations

Speakeasies and bars create downtime and social risk:

- Members relax and spend money.
- Families hold meetings.
- Players play cards or gamble.
- Bartenders hear rumors.
- Rivals observe attendance.
- Police conduct surveillance.
- Hitmen may wait outside or infiltrate.
- A careless phone call may expose plans.
- Owners may declare neutral-ground rules, search entrants, or restrict weapons.

Food and drink can restore stamina, reduce fatigue, affect morale, or cause impairment. Avoid turning hunger into constant busywork.

## 14. Robberies and Criminal Operations

Robberies should be operations, not simple lockpicking progress bars.

Possible flow:

1. Gather intelligence.
2. Select target.
3. Acquire equipment.
4. Assign roles.
5. Execute.
6. Escape.
7. Launder or divide proceeds.
8. Handle evidence and retaliation.

Targets include:

- Stores
- Jewelry shops
- Warehouses
- Banks
- Armored transport
- Rival businesses

NPC robbery reactions depend on:

- Weapon displayed
- Masking
- Aggression
- Reputation
- Witnesses
- Nearby police
- NPC personality
- Whether alarms or cameras exist

Possible reactions:

- Comply
- Stall
- Trigger a silent alarm
- Reach for a weapon
- Flee
- Negotiate
- Lie about safes or keys

## 15. Territory and Influence

Avoid bright capture zones.

Territory emerges through:

- Businesses owned
- Protection rackets
- Crew presence
- Political influence
- Local fear and respect
- Control of logistics
- Police pressure

Territory can be weakened by:

- Attacking income
- Intimidating clients
- Stealing businesses
- Disrupting shipments
- Exposing corruption
- Removing leadership
- Turning informants

## 16. Respect and Fear

Respect and fear are separate.

Respect reflects:

- Reliability
- Honor
- Competence
- Leadership
- Keeping agreements
- Protecting allies

Fear reflects:

- Brutality
- Intimidation
- Ruthlessness
- Retaliation

Different combinations shape NPC reactions and organizational reputation.

## 17. Sit-Downs and Diplomacy

Families can conduct formal sit-downs to negotiate:

- Territory
- Compensation
- Businesses
- Peace
- Prisoners
- Retaliation
- Shared interests

Agreements can be documented or witnessed.

Breaking an agreement damages respect and may justify retaliation.

Violence during a recognized sit-down carries severe social and political consequences.

## 18. Infiltration, Informants, and Corruption

Four possible infiltration models:

### Confidential informant

An existing criminal secretly cooperates with police after arrest, debt, betrayal, or pressure.

Tasks:

- Photograph ledgers
- Identify meetings
- Record conversations
- Mark shipments
- Plant listening devices
- Confirm command structure

Rewards:

- Reduced charges
- Money
- Protection
- Witness relocation

### Corrupt police officer

A police player secretly serves a family by:

- Warning about warrants
- Revealing patrol patterns
- Delaying response
- Misplacing evidence
- Protecting a business
- Exposing informants

### Rival-family mole

A family places an outsider or associate near a rival to observe operations, locations, shipments, and relationships.

### Undercover officer

A later, more complex system requiring:

- Cover identity
- Authorization limits
- Controlled participation in crime
- Evidence collection
- Long-term infiltration
- Strong anti-metagaming safeguards

Sensitive information must be physically observed or stolen. Spies should not gain access merely by joining a team or reading faction chat.

## 19. Police Role and Progression

Police should be interesting because they solve crimes and protect the city, not because they camp every robbery target.

Ranks may include:

- Patrol Officer
- Senior Officer
- Detective
- Sergeant
- Lieutenant
- Captain
- Commissioner

Police progression rewards:

- Preserving evidence
- Solving cases
- Lawful arrests
- Civilian protection
- Successful prosecutions
- Safe informant handling
- Uncovering corruption
- Recovering stolen assets
- Avoiding excessive force

Penalties include:

- Unlawful searches
- Unsupported arrests
- Evidence contamination
- Civilian deaths
- Losing informant identities
- Corruption
- Failed or abusive raids

### Department progression

The police department earns funding and capability as an institution.

Possible unlocks:

- Better communications
- Crime laboratory
- Improved vehicles
- Forensic tools
- Tactical equipment
- Specialized units
- Limited helicopter or NPC support

Individual officers may gain authority, expertise, access, and reputation, but not large combat-stat advantages.

## 20. Evidence and Cases

Police build cases rather than receiving magical wanted levels.

Evidence examples:

- Witness statements
- Fingerprints
- Shell casings
- Weapon matches
- Camera footage
- Vehicle descriptions
- License plates
- Clothing
- Phone records
- Financial ledgers
- Recorded orders
- Marked currency
- Confessions
- Informant testimony

Case progression:

- Unknown suspect
- Person of interest
- Partial identification
- Probable cause
- Warrant
- Arrest
- Prosecution

A street crime may be solved quickly, while dismantling a family requires connecting leadership to multiple operations.

Warrants are authorized by an NPC judge or court system when evidence meets thresholds.

Illegal searches damage departmental standing and may invalidate evidence.

## 21. Population Imbalance and Offline Coverage

Crime must remain possible when no player police are online, but it must never become consequence-free.

Possible baseline city systems:

- NPC patrol response
- Alarms
- Witnesses
- Cameras
- Evidence preservation
- Hospitals
- Courts
- Automated case intake
- Increased future patrol attention

NPC police should be predictable and limited, not superior tactical opponents.

Player police add:

- Investigation
- Interviews
- Informant recruitment
- Warrant preparation
- Surveillance
- Tactical planning
- Major raids
- Prosecution

A city heat or pressure system may escalate NPC attention after repeated serious crime.

Balance should account for population:

- Fewer police online may reduce immediate tactical response.
- Evidence and delayed consequences still accumulate.
- Excess police presence should not make all crime irrational.
- Dynamic city budgets, response delays, political pressure, or diminishing police rewards may prevent one side from dominating.

## 22. Public Opinion and City Stability

A city-level public-opinion value can respond to both factions.

Police gain trust through:

- Protecting civilians
- Solving crimes
- Lawful conduct
- Recovering property

Police lose trust through:

- Brutality
- Corruption
- Failed raids
- Civilian harm

Families may gain local cooperation by:

- Protecting businesses
- Keeping violence controlled
- Providing services
- Honoring agreements

Families lose cooperation through:

- Random terror
- Civilian murders
- Constant public shootouts
- Exploiting their own neighborhoods

Public opinion affects:

- Witness cooperation
- Tips
- Business support
- Recruitment
- Jury behavior
- Political funding
- Private security

## 23. Injury, Incapacitation, and Death

Avoid instant arcade respawning.

Suggested flow:

- Healthy
- Wounded
- Incapacitated
- Stabilized
- Hospital or illegal doctor
- Recovery

Incapacitated players may be:

- Stabilized
- Carried
- Transported
- Arrested
- Rescued
- Deliberately finished

### Confirmed death

Not every defeat permanently kills a character.

A permanent character death requires a deliberate confirmed killing or another clearly terminal circumstance.

Consequences:

- Character is removed from the active season.
- Rank and relationships tied to that character end.
- Family succession may occur.
- Police attention rises.
- Newspaper reports the death if publicly discovered.
- A funeral may occur.
- The player creates a new character.

### Character versus player

- Player account persists.
- Character does not.
- New characters do not automatically inherit rank, relationships, or identity knowledge.
- Cosmetics and account history may persist.

### Rank restoration compromise

The game never automatically restores a dead character's rank.

However, the family may decide to fast-track the player's new character.

Example:

- Anthony Russo, a Capo, is assassinated.
- The player creates Vincent Costa.
- Leadership may recruit Vincent slowly, promote him quickly, or select somebody else.

Rule principle:

> The mechanics do not restore rank; the family decides.

Players should not demand restoration using out-of-character claims. Leadership may consider the real player's reliability because this remains a cooperative game, but the promotion must occur through the family's in-world authority.

## 24. Strategic Reasons for Hits

A hit should produce strategic value and major risk.

Possible reasons:

- Remove a detective building a major case.
- Kill a witness before testimony.
- Eliminate a rival capo before succession.
- Disrupt command structure.
- Retaliate for a broken agreement.
- Remove an unreliable corrupt officer.
- Prevent a major deal.
- Intimidate a neighborhood.
- Create a leadership crisis.
- Stop an informant believed to be compromised.

Costs and consequences:

- Permanent character loss
- Retaliation
- Increased police attention
- Evidence
- Public fear
- Political pressure
- Funeral surveillance
- Succession effects
- Damage to respect if unjustified

## 25. Funerals

Funerals are public social events and intelligence opportunities.

They may involve:

- Mourning
- Leadership appearances
- Rival condolences
- Peace discussions
- Police surveillance
- Identification of associates
- Risk of a second attack
- Newspaper coverage

The funeral home business can become mechanically important.

## 26. Crew AI

Optional NPC crew members have limited traits:

- Loyalty
- Greed
- Competence
- Violence
- Nerve

Commands:

- Follow
- Hold
- Watch a door
- Carry loot
- Drive
- Retreat

Keep AI scope modest and predictable.

## 27. Weapons and Combat Tone

Use a small, period-appropriate arsenal rather than hundreds of weapons.

Potential examples:

- Revolver
- Semi-automatic pistol
- Double-barrel shotgun
- Pump shotgun
- Thompson-style submachine gun
- Bolt-action rifle

Priorities:

- Concealment
- Ammunition scarcity
- Recoil
- Lethality
- Injury
- Evidence
- Social consequences

## 28. Initial MVP Scope

A realistic first version could include:

- One compact neighborhood
- Two to four persistent family institutions
- One police department
- Month-long seasons
- Identity and introductions
- No scoreboard, global chat, or kill feed
- Local voice and text
- Basic phones
- Minimal HUD
- Inventory
- One social club or speakeasy
- Several stores
- One bank
- Basic businesses
- NPC robbery reactions
- Evidence collection
- Case files and warrants
- Family hierarchy and recruitment
- Injury and incapacitation
- Confirmed character death
- Newspaper
- Library archive
- Seasonal history

Potentially defer:

- Full undercover operations
- Advanced wiretapping
- Complex crew AI
- Large vehicle fleet
- Deep court simulation
