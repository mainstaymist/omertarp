OMERTÀ RP — TURN 1

Three directions, one built out

Direction 1a Carbon is the recommendation and is mocked out in full. 1b Vellum and 1c Hairline are specified to the same depth with three sample screens each. Every frame is 1280×720, i.e. 1080p at 2/3 — px values quoted in the notes are the real 1080p values, and each direction states its 1.5× rule.

1a Carbon Recommended. Ink plates, hairline rules, condensed sign-painter caps. The UI behaves like a thing printed on the world, not a thing floating above it.

Bone-warm white instead of #FFF so the UI reads as ink and paper, not glass. Brass marks the one thing under the cursor; #8E2B22 is reserved for what cannot be undone — dropping, drinking, dying. Nothing else is ever coloured.
03 · SPACE, LINE, SHAPE
4pxbase unit. Only 4·8·12·16·24·32·48 exist. 0pxcorner radius, everywhere, no exceptions. 1pxevery rule and border. Never 2px, never doubled. 24pxscreen-edge margin (36px at 1.5×).
Rectangles only, aligned to a 4px grid, so the whole system is expressible as draw.RoundedBox(0,…) and surf.DrawLine.
04 · LEGIBILITY OVER LIVE VIDEO Two techniques, chosen by layer. Nothing gets both.
World layer → 1px hard outline. Four black offsets at 100% alpha, no blur, no soft shadow. A blurred shadow disappears against mid-grey; a hard outline is a shape and cannot.

Cluster layer → scrim plate. Anything with more than three lines (hotbar, verb menu, timed action) sits on #0A0A0B at 62%, 12px padding, one 1px #3A362F rule on the side facing the screen edge. Focus windows go to 94% + full-screen blur, because at that point the player is not playing.

62% ink is dark enough for a sky and light enough to keep the alley visible through it
Icons. White line art, 24px box, 1.5px stroke, tinted #EEEAE1 at rest / #C8A96A when selected / #9A948A at 45% when unavailable. Tint only — never a baked colour, never two tints in one icon.
Motion. Fade only: 120ms in, 220ms out, no slide, no scale. Position is information — a thing that moves while appearing reads as a second thing.
1.5× scale. Everything is expressed in units of the 4px grid, so scaling multiplies the unit, not the layout. Only fixed pixel value in the system is the 1px rule and the 1px text outline — both stay 1px at every scale, because a 1.5px hairline is a grey smear.
Contextual layer on screen for 2–5 seconds, over a moving world

Time is a pulse, not a bar: one 2px #8E2B22 line at the bottom edge that breathes at your heart rate, slowing and shortening as you fade — you feel how long you have without being able to read it, and you can't strategise off it. The closing world is four nested translucent ink rectangles thickening frame by frame (cheap, immediate-mode, no gradient needed). Sound and the copy carry the rest; the text loses words as you go until only one is left.

The newspaper is the one place the system inverts — dark ink on #E8E2D4 paper — because it is a physical object the character is holding, not an overlay. It borrows nothing from the HUD except the mono label voice, and it uses a Didone (Playfair Display) plus a book serif (Crimson Pro) as its own two-file pair. Player deaths, family wars and police activity only become public knowledge here, a day late and slightly wrong, which is the defensible period device: not a typewriter, an editorial desk.

Rules: 1px #1a1713 at 24% for internal divisions, 2px solid for document headers — the one place the system allows a doubled line, because printed matter has a masthead rule. Radius 0. Grid 4px. Icons tinted #1a1713, or #7A2018 when irreversible.
LEGIBILITY — INVERTED
Focus windows are opaque paper at 96%, so contrast is a solved problem — the risk moves to the world layer, where light text on paper cannot exist. World-layer type is therefore #E8E2D4 with the same 1px hard black outline as Carbon, and no plate at all: the contextual HUD reads as chalk, the windows read as paper. Two materials, split by whether the mouse is captured.

LEGIBILITY — BLUR, NOT INK
